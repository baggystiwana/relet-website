Questions

Frequently asked questions

Clear starting points for scoping this service.

What makes an agent job instruction useful?

Include the property address, issue, photographs, occupancy, tenant contact or access route, approval limit if applicable, and the person who should receive updates.

Can one instruction cover a void-property list?

Yes. A room-by-room list can group repairs, clearance, cleaning and presentation items, provided priorities and decision points are clear.

How should extra work found on site be handled?

Record the additional issue and pause that item for approval where it falls outside the agreed scope. Keep the original instruction and the added decision together in the job record.
