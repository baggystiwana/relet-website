Questions

Frequently asked questions

Clear starting points for scoping this service.

Which service should I choose for several different jobs?

Use void turnarounds for a between-tenancy list, landlord repairs for an occupied or focused maintenance issue, or refurbishments for a defined improvement project. Re-Let can clarify overlaps from the brief.

What should I send with a quote request?

Send the postcode, occupancy status, photographs, a prioritised description and any access or decision constraints. For multiple rooms, label photographs by room.

Can cleaning be arranged as part of a wider void scope?

Cleaning and clearance coordination can sit within a wider void plan. The work should be sequenced after disruptive repairs or removal where practical.
